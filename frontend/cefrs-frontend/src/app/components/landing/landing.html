Overview

What is CEFRS?

The Campus Equipment Borrowing & Facility Reservation System streamlines reservations by keeping every request, approval, and notification inside one coordinated experience.

Who Can Use It?

Students, staff, and organizations can browse availability, submit requests, and track progress, while administrators keep resources organized in real time.

Why Choose CEFRS?

Replace verbal requests with a digital workflow so everyone checks availability, sends requests, and receives confirmations from a single trusted system.

With CEFRS you can:

  • Check real-time availability of equipment and facilities
  • Submit borrowing or reservation requests online
  • Receive reservation notifications and reminders
  • Allow administrators to efficiently manage campus resources

Policies & Guidelines

Reservation Policy

Facilities must be reserved at least 24 hours in advance. Reservations are subject to approval by administrators. Cancellations should be made at least 12 hours before the scheduled time.

Equipment Borrowing

Equipment can be borrowed for a maximum period as specified per item. Users are responsible for the condition of borrowed equipment and must return items on time. Late returns may result in penalties.

User Responsibilities

All users must provide accurate information when making requests. Misuse of facilities or equipment may result in account suspension. Users must comply with campus regulations and guidelines.

Approval Process

Requests are reviewed by administrators within 24-48 hours. Approved requests will receive confirmation notifications. Users will be notified of any changes or cancellations.
