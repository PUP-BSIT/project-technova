Overview

What is CEFRS?

The Campus Equipment & Facility Reservation System (CEFRS) is a web-based application designed to simplify the process of borrowing equipment and reserving facilities within educational institutions and organizations.

Who Can Use It?

Our platform serves three main user types: Students who need to reserve facilities or borrow equipment, Organizations managing campus resources, and Administrators who oversee and approve requests.

Why Choose CEFRS?

Replace manual forms and verbal requests with a centralized digital platform. Check real-time availability, submit requests online, receive notifications, and manage resources efficiently—all in one place.

Policies & Guidelines

Reservation Policy

Facilities must be reserved at least 24 hours in advance. Reservations are subject to approval by administrators. Cancellations should be made at least 12 hours before the scheduled time.

Equipment Borrowing

Equipment can be borrowed for a maximum period as specified per item. Users are responsible for the condition of borrowed equipment and must return items on time. Late returns may result in penalties.

User Responsibilities

All users must provide accurate information when making requests. Misuse of facilities or equipment may result in account suspension. Users must comply with campus regulations and guidelines.

Approval Process

Requests are reviewed by administrators within 24-48 hours. Approved requests will receive confirmation notifications. Users will be notified of any changes or cancellations.
